# Website Rebuild Plan for Living Through Learning

## Executive Summary
Living Through Learning (LTL) is a Cape Town-based non-profit organization (NPO/PBO) dedicated to improving foundation phase literacy in disadvantaged schools. This plan outlines a comprehensive strategy to rebuild their websites (https://livingthroughlearning.org.za and https://shop.livingthroughlearning.org.za) from scratch, addressing potential shortcomings in design, functionality, and content while aligning with their mission to uplift educators and learners. The new website will feature a modern design, enhanced user experience, and robust e-commerce capabilities, drawing inspiration from similar organizations like Room to Read and Read to Rise.

## Organization Overview
- **Name**: Living Through Learning (LTL)
- **Location**: 134 Main Rd, Diep River, Cape Town, 7800, South Africa
- **Mission**: To provide training and interventions that support educators and learners in South Africa, focusing on foundation phase literacy to develop reading and writing skills, ignite educators’ passion, and build learners’ confidence.
- **Vision**: “Every child is a national asset and every child must be able to read with meaning.”
- **History**: Established in 1990 (under a different name, formalized as LTL in 2012), with a curriculum used by over 30,000 learners, achieving up to 80% literacy rate improvements.
- **Impact Metrics**:
  - Learners educated: 900
  - Educators trained: 40
  - Students placed in tertiary education: 100
- **Programs**:
  - **Coronation Reading Adventure Room**: A fun, step-by-step English literacy curriculum implemented in 17 schools, impacting 3,000 learners and 105 educators annually.
  - **Educators Forum**: Validates and trains educators to address common teaching challenges.
  - **ACSA Literacy Action Rooms**: Specialized learning environments in four disadvantaged schools.
  - **Teachers in Training**: Training on discipline, behavioral disorders, and classroom management.
- **E-Learning Shop**: Likely offers educational resources like workbooks or digital materials, though specific products are unclear.

## Benchmarking Against Competitors
To inform the rebuild, we analyzed websites of similar literacy-focused non-profits in South Africa:
- **Room to Read (https://www.roomtoread.org/countries/south-africa/)**:
  - **Design**: Clear hierarchy with headings, embedded links, and professional imagery.
  - **Content**: Detailed program descriptions, impact statistics, and calls to action for donations.
  - **Features**: Scalable program details, government partnerships, and a focus on sustainability.
- **Read to Rise (https://www.readtorise.co.za/)**:
  - **Design**: Uses images, captions, and quotes; structured with clear headings and hyperlinks.
  - **Content**: Highlights literacy statistics, mission, campaigns (e.g., Book Ownership Campaign), and testimonials.
  - **Features**: Strong calls to action, awards section, and community engagement focus.

These organizations emphasize clear navigation, impactful visuals, and transparent impact reporting, which LTL’s new website should emulate.

## Design Recommendations
- **Color Scheme**: Use blue and green to evoke education and growth, with accents of yellow for vibrancy.
- **Typography**: Choose legible fonts (e.g., Open Sans for body, Montserrat for headings) with clear hierarchy.
- **Imagery**: Feature high-quality photos of learners, educators, and Reading Adventure Rooms in action.
- **Navigation**: Implement a sticky header with dropdown menus for easy access to all sections.
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Ensure seamless functionality across devices using a responsive framework like Bootstrap.
- **Accessibility**: Adhere to WCAG guidelines, including alt text for images and keyboard navigation support.

## Content Strategy
- **About Us**:
  - Highlight LTL’s 35+ years of experience, phonics-based curriculum, and impact (e.g., “30,000+ learners impacted, 80% literacy rate increase”).
  - Include team bios to personalize the organization.
- **Programs**:
  - Provide detailed descriptions, target audiences, and success metrics for each program.
  - Example: “Coronation Reading Adventure Room: Implemented in 17 schools, improving literacy scores by 10-20% for 3,000 learners annually.”
  - Include testimonials from educators or parents.
- **Shop**:
  - Create clear product categories (e.g., workbooks, digital resources, teacher guides).
  - Ensure secure payment options and detailed product descriptions.
- **Get Involved**:
  - Link to donation platforms like https://www.forgood.co.za.
  - Offer volunteer sign-up forms and partnership inquiry options.
- **News/Blog**:
  - Post regular updates on events (e.g., World Read Aloud Day, Reading Adventure Room launches).
  - Share educational tips and success stories to engage visitors.
- **Contact**:
  - Display the address (134 Main Rd, Diep River, Cape Town, 7800), phone (+27 (0)64 758-4263), and email (reception@livingthroughlearning.org.za).
  - Embed a Google Map showing the location.
  - Include social media links (if available) or placeholders for future accounts.

## E-Commerce Integration
The shop (currently https://shop.livingthroughlearning.org.za) should be integrated into the main website with:
- **Platform**: Use WooCommerce or Shopify for a robust, user-friendly e-commerce experience.
- **Product Listings**: Categorize products (e.g., literacy workbooks, teacher training materials) with clear descriptions, prices, and images.
- **Checkout**: Implement secure payment gateways (e.g., PayFast, Stripe) suitable for South African users.
- **Features**: Add search functionality, filters, and customer reviews to enhance usability.

## SEO and Marketing
- **Keywords**: Optimize for terms like “literacy programs Cape Town,” “education non-profit South Africa,” and “foundation phase literacy.”
- **Meta Tags**: Include descriptive meta titles and descriptions for each page.
- **Local Focus**: Emphasize Cape Town and Western Cape in content to attract local stakeholders.
- **Analytics**: Integrate Google Analytics to track visitor behavior and refine marketing strategies.

## Additional Features
- **Resource Library**: Offer downloadable literacy resources or guides for educators.
- **Event Calendar**: Highlight upcoming workshops or events like International Literacy Day celebrations.
- **Testimonials**: Showcase quotes from learners, educators, or partners to build credibility.
- **Transparency**: Include a section for annual reports or financial statements to demonstrate accountability.
- **Social Media Integration**: Add sharing buttons and, if applicable, live feeds from platforms like X or Facebook.

## Implementation Plan
1. **Content Audit**: Review existing content from both websites to identify reusable material.
2. **Design Phase**: Develop wireframes and mockups using tools like Figma, incorporating the recommended color scheme and typography.
3. **Development**: Build the website using WordPress for flexibility, integrating WooCommerce for the shop.
4. **Testing**: Ensure mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and functionality across browsers.
5. **Launch**: Migrate content from the existing WordPress source code and database, then launch the new site.
6. **Maintenance**: Plan for regular updates, backups, and security checks.

## Conclusion
The rebuilt website will transform Living Through Learning’s online presence into a dynamic, user-friendly platform that effectively communicates their mission, showcases their programs, and drives engagement through a robust e-commerce section and strong SEO. By addressing potential shortcomings in the current websites and incorporating best practices from similar organizations, the new website will support LTL’s goal of awakening Africa through education.
