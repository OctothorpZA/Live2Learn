# Living Through Learning - About Page Content Structure

Based on the current LTL website research and archive documents, here's the complete content structure for the about page.

## Page Structure in Sanity

### Page: About Us (slug: "about")

## InfoSection 1: Hero/Opening
**Heading**: "About Living Through Learning"
**Subheading**: "Awakening Africa through Early Literacy Since 2010"
**Content**:
Living Through Learning (LTL) is a registered NPO/PBO dedicated to transforming foundation phase literacy education across South Africa. Founded by Sonja Botha in 2010, we bring over 35 years of educational excellence to disadvantaged communities, focusing on children aged 4-7 years.

Our comprehensive approach combines the proven Gattegno "Words in Colour" methodology with practical teacher training and innovative learning environments, creating lasting educational impact in Cape Town's townships and beyond.

## InfoSection 2: Our Story
**Heading**: "Our Story"
**Subheading**: "From Education Alive to Living Through Learning"
**Content**:
Founded by Sonja Botha, who previously led Education Alive for over 25 years, Living Through Learning emerged in 2010 as an independent organization with a focused mission. After decades of educational innovation, Sonja recognized the critical need for specialized foundation phase literacy intervention.

Today, LTL has:
- Served over 150,000 learners through literacy programs
- Trained more than 700 educators
- Achieved up to 80% literacy rate increases in participating schools
- Placed over 500 students into tertiary education and meaningful employment
- Developed curriculum used by 30,000+ children across South Africa

## InfoSection 3: Our Vision & Mission
**Heading**: "Our Vision & Mission"
**Subheading**: "Every Child is a National Asset"
**Content**:
**Vision**: "Every child is a national asset and every child must be able to read with meaning."

**Mission**: To provide training and interventions to support educators and learners in South Africa in literacy, giving youth a chance to flourish in their formative schooling years while building educators' passion through practical training.

We believe in developing learners' confidence and career dreams through improved reading and writing comprehension, not just basic skills acquisition.

## InfoSection 4: Our Approach
**Heading**: "The Living Through Learning Methodology"
**Subheading**: "Proven Pedagogy for Foundation Phase Success"
**Content**:
Our unique approach combines:

**Motor Skills Development**: Mastering fine and gross motor skills builds confidence in small children. Our preparatory development activities strengthen muscle control, body strength, and movement skills essential for learning.

**Reading Comprehension**: We focus on understanding and processing information, not just reading ability. Using Gattegno's "Words in Colour" phonics-based methodology, we ensure children read with meaning.

**Accurate Writing**: We teach proper writing techniques from the start to prevent long-term errors and build solid foundations.

**Practical Teacher Training**: Over 35 years of field experience informs our learner-centric, outcome-driven approach that measures both qualitative and quantitative results.

## InfoSection 5: Our Impact
**Heading**: "Transforming Lives Through Literacy"
**Subheading**: "Measurable Results Across South Africa"
**Content**:
**Reading Adventure Rooms (CRAR)**: Our flagship program operates in 30+ schools, benefiting approximately 3,000 learners and 105 educators annually. Schools report average English literacy score increases of 10-20% within a term.

**Recent Success Metrics**:
- 2015 CAPS results increased by 9% from 1st to 2nd term in CRAR schools
- Overall average increase of 18% from baseline to Term 2 assessments
- Students who couldn't speak English now confidently participate in conversations
- Enhanced interactive learning through technology integration

**Educator Forums**: Growing from 30 to 250+ participants, our professional development sessions cover classroom management, communication, lesson planning, and learning barriers.
