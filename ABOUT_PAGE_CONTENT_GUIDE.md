# About Page Content Guide for Living Through Learning

## Overview
This guide provides content structure recommendations for the LTL About page to align with organizational context and project plans. The about page uses a modular PageBuilder system with Sanity CMS, allowing flexible content management.

## Recommended Page Structure

### 1. Hero/Opening Section (InfoSection)
**Heading**: "About Living Through Learning"
**Subheading**: "Empowering Early Literacy in South Africa Since 1990"
**Content**: 
- Brief overview of LTL's mission and 35+ years of impact
- Core focus on foundation phase literacy (ages 4-7)
- Geographic focus: Cape Town and broader South Africa

### 2. Our Story (InfoSection)
**Heading**: "Our Story"
**Subheading**: "From Vision to Impact"
**Content**:
- Founded in 1990, formalized as LTL in 2012 by Sonja Botha
- Vision: "Every child is a national asset and every child must be able to read with meaning"
- Evolution from local initiative to impacting 30,000+ learners
- Location: 134 Main Rd, Diep River, Cape Town

### 3. Our Methodology (InfoSection)
**Heading**: "The Gattegno Approach"
**Subheading**: "Evidence-Based Phonics for Foundation Phase"
**Content**:
- Gattegno's "Words in Colour" phonics-based methodology
- Step-by-step English literacy curriculum
- Achieving up to 80% literacy rate improvements
- Focus on ages 4-7 (foundation phase)

### 4. Our Impact (InfoSection)
**Heading**: "Transforming Lives Through Literacy"
**Subheading**: "Measurable Impact Across South Africa"
**Content**:
- 35+ years of educational expertise
- 30,000+ learners reached through curriculum
- 3,000+ learners and 105 educators impacted annually through CRAR
- 17 schools currently implementing Reading Adventure Rooms

### 5. Team Grid Section (TeamGrid)
**Headline**: "Meet Our Dedicated Team"
**Intro**: "Our passionate educators, facilitators, and literacy specialists are committed to transforming foundation phase education across South Africa."

## Team Member Profiles to Include

### Key Leadership Roles:
1. **Executive Director/Founder**
   - Role: Executive Director & Founder
   - Specialty: Gattegno Methodology, Foundation Phase Literacy
   - Focus: 35+ years experience, curriculum development

2. **Program Coordinators**
   - Role: CRAR Program Coordinator
   - Specialty: Reading Adventure Room Implementation
   - Focus: School partnership management

3. **Literacy Facilitators**
   - Role: Senior Literacy Facilitator
   - Specialty: Teacher Training, Classroom Implementation
   - Focus: Educator support and training

4. **Training Specialists**
   - Role: Professional Development Specialist
   - Specialty: Behavioral Management, Teaching Strategies
   - Focus: Teachers in Training program

### Content Guidelines for Team Bios:
- Highlight experience with foundation phase literacy
- Include years with LTL (if applicable)
- Mention specialization in Gattegno method or related approaches
- Focus on impact with educators and learners
- Include relevant educational background or certifications

## Technical Implementation

### Sanity Studio Setup:
1. **Create About Page**: 
   - Slug: `about`
   - Name: "About Us"
   - Use PageBuilder with InfoSection and TeamGrid blocks

2. **Team Member Creation**:
   - Use Person document type
   - Include professional headshots (400x400 minimum)
   - Set displayOrder for leadership hierarchy
   - Complete bio with LTL context

3. **Content Best Practices**:
   - Keep InfoSection content scannable with subheadings
   - Use bullet points for impact metrics
   - Include internal links to programs and contact pages
   - Optimize images for web (WebP format recommended)

### Component Features:
- **InfoSection**: Fully responsive with LTL brand colors
- **TeamGrid**: Professional team member display with hover effects
- **Mobile Optimization**: Responsive grid adjusts for all screen sizes
- **Accessibility**: Alt text for images, semantic HTML structure

## SEO Considerations
- **Page Title**: "About Living Through Learning - Foundation Phase Literacy Experts"
- **Meta Description**: "Learn about Living Through Learning's 35+ years of impact in South African foundation phase literacy education using the proven Gattegno methodology."
- **Keywords**: Foundation phase literacy, South African education, Gattegno method, reading intervention, Cape Town NPO

## Content Management Workflow
1. Edit content directly in Sanity Studio at `/studio`
2. Use Live Preview to see changes in real-time
3. Team members can be added/updated independently
4. Content changes reflect immediately on published site
5. Version history maintained in Sanity

This structure aligns with LTL's organizational goals while maintaining flexibility for future updates and ensuring all content is manageable through Sanity CMS.
